# TradingGame2

Market Day is a standalone stock and instruments trading game that runs in your browser. Buy and sell simulated stocks, an index fund, gold, and Bitcoin while tracking your cash, portfolio value, and holdings.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation, account, or internet connection is required. Prices move every five seconds, and your game is saved locally in the browser. Choose **New game** to reset to $10,000.

All instruments and prices are fictional game data; trades do not involve real money or financial markets.
